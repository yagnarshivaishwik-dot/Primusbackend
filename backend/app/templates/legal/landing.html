What we do

Three layers, one platform.

Primus runs the boring part of running a gaming café — so the operators can focus on the room, the events, and the players.

01

Kiosk

A locked-down Windows client with a touch-friendly React UI. One tap to log in, browse packages, top up time, and start a session.

02

Inventory

Café operators define time packages — duration, pricing, bonuses, happy-hour windows — and they sync to every kiosk in real time.

03

Payments

UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets — all through Cashfree, with HMAC-signed webhooks and idempotent crediting. Time hits the account in seconds.
