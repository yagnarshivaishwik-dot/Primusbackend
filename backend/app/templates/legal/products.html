How it works

Five taps, one play session.

  1. Walk up to a Primus kiosk at any partner café.
  2. Sign in with your Primus account, or register on the spot in 20 seconds.
  3. Pick a package, add to cart, tap Pay.
  4. Complete the payment on the secure Cashfree screen — UPI, card, netbanking, wallets.
  5. Time is credited instantly. Walk to your station.
